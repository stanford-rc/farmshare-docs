# Adding Capacity

FarmShare is a shared resource with limited dedicated funding, and we appreciate any contributions that the community can make. For example...

* If you are using FarmShare to teach a class, let your department chair know!
* If you are a registered student group using FarmShare, let your faculty representative and/or the ASSU know!
* If you would like to contribute funds for additional capacity, [contact us](mailto:srcc-support@stanford.edu?subject=FarmShare funds contribution) and we can arrange either an iJournals transfer to our PTA, or a capital purchase from your PTA.
* If you already have hardware being supported by SRC, FarmShare may be able to use that hardware once you are done with it.

We appreciate anything you can do to get the word out about FarmShare and how awesome it is!
