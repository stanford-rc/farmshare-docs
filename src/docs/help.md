# Getting Help

## Join the Community

FarmShare is on [Slack](https://uit.stanford.edu/service/slack)! Join us on:

- [`#farmshare-announce`](https://stanford.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C018K0RR3GU), a low-volume channel for important announcements.
- [`#farmshare-users`](https://stanford.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C018638T170), a channel where users can connect directly with one another.

## Submit a Support Ticket

Most FarmShare support is provided during business hours. To report a problem or get help, send e-mail to [srcc-support@stanford.edu](mailto:srcc-support@stanford.edu). Make sure you have “FarmShare” somewhere in the subject line, and please be as detailed as possible with your request.

## Report Accessibility Issues

Stanford is committed to providing an accessible digital environment. If you experience accessibility barriers on this site, please visit [Digital Accessibility at Stanford](https://www.stanford.edu/site/accessibility).
